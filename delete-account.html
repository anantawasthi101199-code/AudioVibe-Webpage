Delete your account

You can delete your AudioVibe account and the data associated with it at any time. This page explains exactly what is removed, what is kept, and the two ways to ask.

Service: AudioVibe  ·  Developer: Alpha Tech Labs Ltd  ·  Package: co.audiovibe
01

Delete it yourself, in the app

This is the fastest route and it takes effect immediately.

  1. Open AudioVibe and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and content are removed straight away.

Deleting your account is permanent. It cannot be undone, and your username is not reserved for you afterwards.

02

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, or you cannot sign in, email us and we will delete the account for you.

Email
generalinquiries@audiovibe.co

Subject
Account deletion request

Please include:

  • The email address on the account, or your @username
  • Confirmation that you want the account and its data deleted

We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act, so that we do not delete someone else's account on a false request. We will complete the deletion and confirm it to you within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

What is kept, and why

A small amount of data survives deletion. We keep only what we have to, and only for as long as we have to.

DataWhyHow long
Messages you already sent to another personThey are part of that person's conversation too, and we cannot delete someone else's copy of a conversation they took part inUntil that person deletes them
Moderation and enforcement records, where your account was reported or actionedTo identify repeat behaviour and to meet our duties under the Online Safety Act 2023Up to 24 months after the case closes
Server and security logsSecurity, fraud investigation and service integrityUp to 90 days
Aggregated statistics that no longer identify youUnderstanding how the service is usedIndefinitely, in a form that cannot be linked back to you
Records we are legally required to keepLegal obligationFor the period the law requires
05

Want a copy of your data first?

Deletion is permanent, so if you want a copy of what we hold about you, ask before you delete. Email generalinquiries@audiovibe.co with the subject "Data access request" and we will send it within one month, free of charge.

Your other data protection rights, and how to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, are set out in our Privacy Policy.
