04

Recommendations and profiling

AudioVibe builds a picture of what you like from what you listen to, finish, skip, like, save and follow, and uses it to order the For You feed, the Discover rails and creator suggestions. This is profiling as the UK GDPR defines it, so we want to be plain about it.

  • It affects what content is shown to you and in what order. It does not affect your access to the service, your account standing, or anything with a legal or similarly significant effect on you.
  • There is no automated decision-making under Article 22. Moderation decisions that restrict an account are made or reviewed by a person.
  • We do not use your data to build an advertising profile, and we do not sell it.
  • You can object to profiling for recommendations by contacting us. The feed then falls back to non-personalised ordering.

08

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the following rights. They are free to exercise, and we will respond within one month.

  • Access. Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification. Have inaccurate data corrected. Most of it you can edit yourself in the app.
  • Erasure. Ask us to delete your data. See section 9.
  • Restriction. Ask us to pause processing while a dispute is resolved.
  • Portability. Receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable format, or have it sent to another provider where technically feasible.
  • Objection. Object to processing based on legitimate interests, including profiling for recommendations.
  • Withdraw consent. Where we rely on consent, such as push notifications, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting what we did before.
  • Complain. Raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office. See section 13.

To exercise any of these, email us at generalinquiries@audiovibe.co. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act, so that we do not disclose your data to someone else.

09

Deleting your account and your data

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: Settings, then Delete account. Deletion removes your profile, your audio, series, playlists, echoes, comments, likes, follows, listening history and the underlying files in our object storage.

If you cannot or do not want to install the app, you can request deletion instead. Full details of what is removed, what is kept and how to ask are on our account deletion page.

Uninstalling the app stops further collection from your device, but it does not delete the account. Use one of the two routes above for that.

10

Children and young people

You must be at least 13 to use AudioVibe. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. If we learn that we have, we delete the account and the data.

We know that some of our users are between 13 and 17, and we design for that. We follow the Information Commissioner's Office Age Appropriate Design Code, which means among other things:

  • Content rated 18+ is hidden by default, and can only be turned on by an account whose declared date of birth is 18 or over.
  • We collect the minimum data we need, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not use nudge techniques to push you toward weaker privacy settings.
  • We do not collect precise location.

Age is currently established from the date of birth you declare when you sign up. If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about a young person's account, contact us and we will help.

11

How we protect it

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS, with HTTP Strict Transport Security enforced.
  • Passwords are stored as salted hashes. We never store or transmit them in plain text, and we cannot read them.
  • Sign-in tokens are held in the operating system keychain or keystore on your device, not in ordinary app storage, and are excluded from device backups.
  • Access tokens are short-lived. Refresh tokens rotate on every use, and reuse of an old token revokes the whole session family.
  • Data at rest is encrypted by our hosting and storage providers.
  • Rate limiting, request validation and abuse detection protect the service from automated attack.
  • Access to production data inside Alpha Tech Labs Ltd is limited to those who need it, and administrative actions on user data are recorded in an audit log.

No online service can promise perfect security. If we ever suffer a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will report it to the Information Commissioner's Office within 72 hours and tell you where the law requires it.
