Built to be heard, not stared at

These are real screens from the app. Scroll, and move your cursor over the phone.

A feed you listen to

For You plays one thing after another and learns from what you actually finish. Put the phone in your pocket and it keeps going.

Controls where your hands are

Pause, skip and jump back ten seconds from the lock screen, the notification shade or your headphones.

Series that keep going

Follow a series and it moves on to the next episode by itself, then tells you when a new one lands.

Pick a lane

Stories, news, comedy, science and tech, health, history. Browse by category instead of taking whatever comes next.

Falls asleep with you

Choose a time, or the end of the episode. The audio fades out and stops on its own.

Every voice has a home

Follow a creator and their new work rises to the top of your feed instead of getting buried.

  • Voice notes and shared audio in messages
  • Downloads in folders, for listening offline
  • Playlists you can copy from friends

AudioVibe Originals

Open the channel

Use the left and right arrow keys when the ring is focused.

Make your own

If you have something worth hearing, you can publish it from your phone. Listeners stay to the end, because they came to listen.

Creator accounts open with an invite code. Ask for a code and tell us what you'd make.

  1. Record and publish

    Record in the app or upload a file, add a title and cover art, and it's live.

  2. Group episodes into a series

    Listeners follow the series, and the next episode reaches them on its own.

  3. Share one link

    Every audio, series and profile has a link that unfurls into a preview card and opens straight in the app.
